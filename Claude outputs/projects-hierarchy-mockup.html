02 · go · postgres

Taskq

A durable job queue on Postgres alone: atomic claiming, leases with fencing, retries with backoff, a reaper for crashed workers, and a transactional outbox.

0.19 msclaim on 1M rows
4,383jobs/s, 32 workers
code ↗benchmarks →
// what I learned draft, your words
  1. A full index was slower than no index. A partial index on queued jobs took the claim from ~306 ms to 0.19 ms.
  2. Durability has a price: ~80% of each job's time was the commit flushing to disk.
  3. Exactly-once isn't possible here, so handlers have to be idempotent.
